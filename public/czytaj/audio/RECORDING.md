# Human replacement guide

Any clip can replace its AI version without an application-code change. Keep the same audio ID and filename, then set that manifest entry to `"source": "human"`.

- Record mono audio in a quiet, non-reverberant room.
- Keep the microphone distance and speaking volume consistent.
- Use natural Polish, slightly slower than conversation.
- Trim leading and trailing silence without clipping consonants.
- Normalize all clips to a consistent perceived loudness.
- For grapheme clips, pronounce the sound, not the alphabetic name, and do not add a vowel.
- A native Polish reviewer must approve pronunciation, stress, `ą`, `ę`, soft consonants, digraphs, pace, artifacts and silence.
- After three failed AI generations, use a reviewed human replacement before releasing that item.
